How it works

Plug in, and it just works. Unplug, and it keeps going.

One app does both jobs. Install Flowshare on the phone that shares and on the device that receives: another Android phone or tablet, a Windows PC, or a TV.

1

Install on both

Put Flowshare on the phone that shares and on the device that receives: another Android phone or tablet, a Windows PC, or a Google TV.

2

Start sharing

Open Flowshare on your phone and tap Start sharing.

3

Plug in

Connect a USB data cable. Flowshare pairs over the cable, so later it can also connect without one: unplug, and it switches to Wi-Fi Direct by itself.

Features

Built for cloud gaming. Great for everything else.

Game streams have almost no buffer, so every millisecond of jitter shows. Flowshare is designed around that.

Game traffic goes first

Cloud games like GeForce NOW and Xbox Cloud Gaming get priority over downloads, with an optional game list, reserved bandwidth and a priority buffer.

USB or Wi-Fi Direct, automatically

Automatic mode uses the cable when it's plugged in and switches to Wi-Fi Direct when it isn't. No router needed.

Starts by itself

Turn on the TV or plug in the phone and Flowshare reconnects on its own. No buttons to press.

Low-latency engine

A native networking core keeps the phone's modem awake, avoids batching delays and charges the phone over USB while it shares.

Know your signal

See 5G or 4G, signal strength, latency and live speed, plus tips when your signal, not your setup, is the problem.

Private by design

The tunnel runs entirely on your devices. Your traffic never goes to us. No accounts, no ads, no analytics SDKs.

FAQ

Questions

What do I need?

An Android phone with mobile data, and a Google TV or Android TV running Android 10 or newer (a tablet or second phone also works as the receiver). For USB mode, a USB data cable from the TV's USB port to the phone. Charge-only cables won't work. A powered USB hub helps keep the phone charged.

Does it use my mobile data?

Yes. Everything the TV does goes over your phone's mobile connection and counts toward your plan. Cloud gaming in HD can use several GB per hour. Check your plan's data allowance and terms before you use Flowshare.

Is this allowed by my carrier?

That depends on your plan. Some plans limit how a phone's connection can be shared. You are responsible for following your carrier's terms. Flowshare does not change what your plan allows.

Is Flowshare a VPN? Does my traffic go through your servers?

No. On the TV, Flowshare uses Android's VPN feature only to create a local tunnel to your phone over the cable or Wi-Fi Direct. There is no Flowshare server. Your traffic goes from the TV to your phone and straight to the internet. We never see it.

USB or Wi-Fi Direct: which is better?

USB is the smoothest and lowest-latency, and it charges the phone. Wi-Fi Direct needs no cable and is great for video. Automatic mode uses USB when plugged in and Wi-Fi Direct otherwise.

Which games work?

Cloud gaming services such as GeForce NOW and Xbox Cloud Gaming. Game traffic is recognized and sent first. Results depend on your mobile signal. Moving the phone toward a window helps more than anything else.

Why does it ask for location or nearby devices?

Android requires the "Nearby devices" permission (or location on Android 12 and older) for any app that uses Wi-Fi Direct. Flowshare never reads or stores your location.

I bought Pro. How do I get it on a new device?

Install Flowshare from Google Play with the same Google account and open Settings → Restore purchase.

What data does Flowshare collect?

None by default on Google Play. If you turn on diagnostics, connection statistics (speed, signal, latency, device model, app version) are sent to help fix stutters. See the privacy policy.

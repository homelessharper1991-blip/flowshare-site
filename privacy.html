Flowshare Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 11, 2026

This policy explains what the Flowshare app (Android package com.flowshare.host) and its experimental router, Windows and Mac versions do with your information. The short version: Flowshare has no accounts, no ads and no analytics SDKs. Your internet traffic never goes to us. The only data that can reach the developer is optional connection diagnostics, which you control.

Questions: our support email (available at launch)

1. What Flowshare does

Flowshare lets one device (usually a phone) share its mobile internet with another device (a Google TV / Android TV, tablet, second phone, or a router or computer running the experimental versions) over a USB cable or Wi-Fi Direct.

On the receiving device, Flowshare uses Android's built-in VPN feature (VpnService) to create a local tunnel that carries that device's traffic over the cable or Wi-Fi Direct link to the sharing phone, which sends it to the internet over its own mobile connection. There is no Flowshare VPN server. Flowshare does not read, record, filter, block, modify or sell the content of your traffic, and none of it is sent to the developer.

2. Information stored only on your devices

These stay on your device and are never sent to us:

  • Your settings (connection mode, Wi-Fi band, game priority list and similar options).
  • The Wi-Fi Direct pairing code, used so your TV can find your phone.
  • Your free-plan usage timer and whether Flowshare Pro is unlocked.

Uninstalling the app deletes them. Flowshare turns off Android cloud backup for its data, so none of this is copied to your Google account.

3. Diagnostics (optional)

To help fix stutters and connection problems, Flowshare can send connection diagnostics to the developer.

What is sent: connection statistics such as transfer speeds, mobile network type (5G/4G) and signal strength, latency and jitter, cable round-trip time, number of open connections, dropped links, CPU use of the app, device temperature/thermal state, connection events and error messages (for example "link up", "permission denied"), the device manufacturer and model, the Android version and the Flowshare version. If the app crashes, a technical crash report (error type, where in the code it happened, app version and device model, with addresses and long numbers masked) is saved on the device and sent on the next launch only if diagnostics are on; otherwise it is deleted.

What is never sent: the websites, apps or content you use, your traffic, your phone number, contacts, accounts, precise or approximate location, advertising ID, or any name or email address.

When it is sent: while sharing, the app keeps the last 90 seconds of statistics in memory. They are sent only when diagnostics are on and either you tap "It just stuttered" or "Send diagnostics", game data stalls (at most once every 3 minutes), or a connection event happens.

Your choice:

  • Copies installed from Google Play: diagnostics (including crash reports) are off until you turn on "Share diagnostics with the developer" in Settings.
  • Copies installed directly (APK download from our website or GitHub) are test builds: diagnostics are on by default so problems can be fixed quickly. You can turn them off at any time in Settings.

How it is delivered: diagnostics are posted over HTTPS to a private channel on ntfy.sh, a third-party notification relay. Like any web service, ntfy.sh receives your device's IP address with the request; see the ntfy.sh privacy policy. ntfy.sh keeps messages for a short time (about 12 hours by default). The developer reads them to fix bugs and may keep notes from them for up to 12 months. Diagnostics are not linked to your identity, not sold, not shared with anyone else and not used for advertising.

4. Purchases

Flowshare Pro is a one-time in-app purchase handled entirely by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment under the Google Privacy Policy. The developer receives only what Google provides to every developer (an order record without your card details) and does not receive your payment information. The app asks Google Play whether Pro was bought, so the unlock can be restored on a new device.

5. Updates for directly installed copies

Copies installed outside Google Play check GitHub (github.com/homelessharper1991-blip/flowshare) about every 6 hours for a newer version and download it from GitHub. GitHub receives your IP address with these requests, under the GitHub Privacy Statement. Copies from Google Play are updated by Google Play instead.

7. Experimental versions (router, Windows, Mac)

The experimental OpenWrt router, Windows and Mac versions work the same way: they route traffic over the cable to the phone running Flowshare, keep their settings locally, and do not send your traffic to the developer. They do not include analytics.

8. Children

Flowshare is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Security

Diagnostics are sent over encrypted HTTPS. Traffic between your devices stays on the USB cable or the encrypted Wi-Fi Direct (WPA2) link between them. No method of transmission is perfectly secure, but we collect as little as possible so there is little to protect.

10. Your rights

Because diagnostics are not tied to a name, account or email address, we usually cannot identify which messages came from you. You can stop all diagnostics at any time in Settings, or uninstall the app to delete everything stored on your device. To ask about or request deletion of diagnostics, email our support email (available at launch) with your device model and the approximate time; we will delete matching records we hold. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have additional rights to access or erase data; contact us to use them. We do not sell or share personal information as defined by the CCPA.

11. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and post the new version on our website. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's release notes.
